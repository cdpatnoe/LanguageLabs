# Italian Language Lab (Laboratorio di Italiano) 🇮🇹

An interactive, 100% offline-capable language learning studio for **Italian (Italiano)** built on the **Pluggable Language Pack Architecture** (`lang-config.js`).

## ✨ Features & Three Learning Studios

### 1. ⚡ Verb Lab (*Verbi*)
- **20 Essential Italian Verbs** organized into 5 clusters (*The Big 4 Irregulars*, *Modals*, *-are Regulars*, *-ere Verbs*, and *-ire & -isc- Verbs*).
- **Passato Prossimo Auxiliary (*Essere* vs. *Avere*)**: Full conjugation matrices for *Presente Indicativo*, *Imperfetto*, *Passato Prossimo*, and *Imperativo* (*Tu* / *Lei* / *Voi*) — with **written phonetic guides and 🔊 audio buttons** on every conjugation cell.
- **SRS Flashcards, Speed Match & Targeted Quizzes**: Drill conjugation, *Essere* vs. *Avere* auxiliary rules, and imperatives.
- **My Notes & Smart Parser**: Paste or upload `.txt`, `.md`, `.csv`, or `.rtf` study notes with automatic Italian phonetic guide generation.

### 2. 📖 Vocabulary Studio (*Vocabolario*)
- **67 Core Italian Words & Expressions** across 10 categories, including **🌶️ Swearing & Slang (*Parolacce e Slang*)**.
- **Grammatical Gender (*Maschile* / *Femminile*)**: Every noun and adjective is annotated with gender, definite articles (`il/lo/l'/la` → `i/gli/le`), and plural forms.
- **6 Built-in Targeted Quiz Packs**: *Days of the Week*, *Question Words*, *Time & Frequency*, *Directions & Positions*, *Numbers 1–10*, and *🌶️ Swearing & Slang*, plus custom quiz creation.

### 3. 🔤 Sounds & Pairs (*Pronuncia & Doppie*)
- **24 Graduated Sound & Digraph Words** covering `c/ci/ce` vs `ch`, `g/gi/ge` vs `gh`, `gli` & `gn`, `sce/sci` vs `sche/schi`, double consonants (*Le Doppie*), and `z/zz` + trilled `rr`.
- **10 Minimal Contrast Pairs** (*capello* vs *cappello*, *pesce* vs *pesche*, *pala* vs *palla*, *casa* vs *cassa*, *figlio* vs *filo*, *sogno* vs *sono*, etc.).
- **Native `it-IT` Web Speech Synthesis**: Normal speed (`1.0x`), Slow (`0.7x`), and Syllable-by-Syllable breakdown.
