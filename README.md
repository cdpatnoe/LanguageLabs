# Hindi Language Lab (हिन्दी अभ्यासशाला)
### Triple Studio Practice: Verb Lab (क्रियाशाला), Vocabulary Studio (शब्दशाला) & Matra Reading Lab (मात्रा वाचन)

A comprehensive, 100% offline local web application designed to help English speakers master **Hindi Verbs & Conjugation**, **Core Vocabulary (Nouns, Adjectives, Agreement)**, and **Devanagari Matra Reading Fluency & Phonics**.

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally & Offline

Because this application is built with modern vanilla web standards, **no build steps, Node packages, web servers, or cloud dependencies are required**:

### Option 1: On Mac
From your terminal:
```bash
open /Users/patnoe/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/hindi-verb-lab/index.html
```
Or simply double-click `index.html` in Finder to open in Safari, Chrome, or Edge.

### Option 2: On Windows
1. Double-click `index.html` in Windows File Explorer to open in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
2. **Text-to-Speech Voice Pack (Optional but recommended):**
   - Windows 10 & 11 include high-quality Indic speech synthesis.
   - If Hindi pronunciation does not speak immediately, enable it via:
     `Windows Settings > Time & Language > Speech > Add voices > Select "Hindi (India)"` (e.g., *Microsoft Hemant* or *Microsoft Kalpana*).
3. **Indic Font Rendering:** The app uses `"Nirmala UI"` and `"Mangal"`, the official OpenType Devanagari fonts preinstalled on all Windows systems, ensuring sharp, conjunct-accurate Devanagari rendering.

## 🌟 The Three Learning Studios

When launching the app, you can choose your focus or switch between studios anytime using the header toggle or by pressing <kbd>M</kbd>:

### ⚡ 1. Verb Lab (क्रियाशाला)
* **38 Curated Verbs:** The Big 4 Irregulars (*करना, होना, देना, लेना*), Motion, Daily Essentials, Speech, Cognition, and Nominal Compounds.
* **3-Tense Multi-Sentence Review:** Beginner-friendly learner sentences for **Present Habitual**, **Current Continuous**, and **Simple Past**.
* **Ergative 'ने' Rule Mastery:** Transitivity rules, irregular stems (e.g. *किया, गया, दिया, लिया*), and gender agreement with direct objects.
* **Imperative Command Forms:** Intimate (तू), Familiar (तुम), and Respectful (आप).
* **Verb Tools:** Explorer with Cluster & 'ने' filters, 3D Flashcards with tense cycling (<kbd>T</kbd>), Speed Match drill, and 'ने' Tense Quiz.

### 📚 2. Vocabulary Studio (शब्दशाला)
* **71 Essential Words:** Core semester 1 & 2 vocabulary spanning **People & Family**, **Home & Everyday**, **Food & Dining**, **Places & Nature**, **Descriptive Adjectives**, **Colors**, and **Postpositions**.
* **Grammatical Gender Mastery:** Every noun is marked with **Masculine (पुल्लिंग - ♂)** or **Feminine (स्त्रीलिंग - ♀)** with clear visual distinction.
* **Plural & Oblique Cases:** Complete tables for Direct Plurals (e.g., *लड़का → लड़के*, *किताब → किताबें*, *कुर्सी → कुर्सियाँ*) and Oblique forms before postpositions (e.g., *कमरे में*, *लड़कों को*).
* **Adjective-Noun Agreement:** Clear distinction between **Inflecting marked adjectives** (e.g. *बड़ा/बड़ी/बड़े*) and **Invariable unmarked adjectives** (e.g. *साफ़, सुंदर*).
* **Vocabulary Tools:** Explorer with POS and Gender filters, 3D Flashcards with plural/inflection revelations, Speed Match drill, and dynamic **Gender & Agreement Quiz**.

### 🔤 3. Matra Reading Practice (मात्रा वाचन अभ्यास)
* **96 Curated Words With Visible Matras (+ 10 Foundational Words Without Matras):** Complete graduated curriculum covering:
  - **अ (Mukta / No Matra • Foundational):** Inherent schwa and final consonant truncation (*कल, घर, जल, शहर*). Accessible via its dedicated filter.
  - **आ (ा):** Standing stroke, open 'ā' sound (*आम, काम, बात, रात*).
  - **इ (ि - Chhoti I):** Pre-consonantal short 'i' (*दिन, किताब, मित्र, रवि*).
  - **ई (ी - Badi I):** Post-consonantal long 'ī' (*पानी, नदी, हाथी, लड़की*).
  - **उ (ु - Chhota U):** Left curl short 'u' (*पुल, मुख, खुशी, बहुत*).
  - **ऊ (ू - Bada U):** Right curl long 'ū' (*दूध, फूल, सूरज, आलू*).
  - **ऋ (ृ):** Vocalic 'ṛ' semi-circle (*गृह, कृपा, ऋषि, अमृत*).
  - **ए (े):** Single top diagonal flag (*सेब, पेड़, केला, खेल*).
  - **ऐ (ै):** Double top diagonal flags (*पैसा, कैसा, बैल, सैनिक*).
  - **ओ (ो):** Standing bar + single flag (*मोर, तोता, रोटी, सोना*).
  - **औ (ौ):** Standing bar + double flags (*पौधा, औरत, नौका, मौसम*).
  - **अं (ं - Anusvara):** Nasal dot on headline (*रंग, पतंग, मंदिर, जंगल*).
  - **अँ (ँ - Anunasika / Chandrabindu):** Crescent nasal dot (*आँख, चाँद, गाँव, माँ*).
  - **आधे अक्षर (् - Conjuncts / Half Letters):** Virama conjuncts (*बच्चा, दोस्त, अच्छा, प्यार*).
* **Recitation & Pronunciation Flashcards:**
  - Front: Prominent Devanagari text, matra badge, breakdown hint preview, and hero **Listen / Pronounce button** (<kbd>A</kbd>). Recite mentally, click or press <kbd>A</kbd> to have the card speak the native audio, and flip (<kbd>Space</kbd> / Click) to verify.
  - Back: Letter math equation (e.g. *क + ि + त + ा + ब = किताब*), phonics positioning rule, English meaning, and collocated example phrase with audio (<kbd>P</kbd>).
  - Deliberate Practice Pills Bar: Scope flashcards to drill any specific matra family, the full 96-word active matras deck, or foundational words without matra.
  - Auto-Speak Toggle: Hands-free automatic audio pronunciation on card advance and flip.
* **Matra Tools:** Word Bank with length and matra filters, Speed Match drill with instant audio, and a 10-question **Sound & Reading Quiz** (including listening comprehension questions).

## ♿ High Accessibility (WCAG AA) & Keyboard Controls

* **Switch Learning Focus:** Press <kbd>M</kbd> to open the Practice Focus Chooser or switch between Verb Lab, Vocabulary Studio, and Matra Reading.
* **Data Cleanup & Lexicon Manager:** Press <kbd>D</kbd> from anywhere to open the Lexicon Manager to review, audit, edit, deduplicate, or delete words.
* **WAI-ARIA Tab Navigation:** Navigate the main study tabs using <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> / <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> keys.
* **Flashcard SRS Shortcuts:**
  * <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd>: Flip between front and back.
  * <kbd>T</kbd>: Cycle verb sentence tenses (Present &rarr; Current &rarr; Past &rarr; All 3) *(in Verb Lab)*.
  * <kbd>E</kbd>: Listen to the current example sentence audio pronunciation.
  * <kbd>A</kbd> (or <kbd>L</kbd>): Listen to native Hindi word audio pronunciation.
  * <kbd>←</kbd> (or <kbd>P</kbd>): Previous card.
  * <kbd>→</kbd> (or <kbd>N</kbd>): Next card.
  * <kbd>1</kbd>: Mark "Needs Review" (↺).
  * <kbd>2</kbd>: Mark "Mastered" (✓).
  * <kbd>S</kbd>: Shuffle deck.
* **Speed Match Keyboard Navigation:** Tiles are fully focusable with <kbd>Tab</kbd>; press <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> to select and match.
* **Modal Dialog Focus Trap:** Opening any verb conjugation sheet or word inspector traps keyboard focus within the dialog; press <kbd>Escape</kbd> to return to your previous spot.
* **Screen Reader Announcer:** Dynamic polite live region (`#a11y-announcer`) announces card flips, match successes, filter results, and quiz explanations for JAWS, NVDA, Windows Narrator, and VoiceOver.
* **Windows High Contrast Mode:** Full support for Windows Forced Colors / High Contrast themes (*Aquatic*, *Desert*, *Dusk*, *Night Sky*).

## 🛡️ Error Robustness & Windows Quirks Handled

* **Speech Synthesis Engine Hardening:** Solves browser garbage collection and `cancel()` race conditions in Chrome, Edge, and Safari by preserving active utterance references in a window-level registry, un-pausing frozen engines, and deploying a watchdog timer to guarantee reliable repeated playback.
* **SafeStorage Resilience:** Seamless fallback to memory storage if `localStorage` is disabled, blocked in private browsing, or reaches disk quota.
* **Non-Blocking Toast System:** User-friendly alert and toast notifications instead of jarring, tab-freezing `alert()` dialogs.
* **Windows UTF-8 BOM Stripping:** Automatically removes Byte Order Marks (`\uFEFF`) often prepended by Windows Notepad or Excel.
* **Line-Ending Normalization:** Transparently accepts both Windows CRLF (`\r\n`) and Unix LF (`\n`) in all note inputs.
* **Drag-and-Drop Navigation Guard:** Prevents the Windows Edge/Chrome default behavior where dropping a file outside a zone causes the browser to navigate away from the app.

## 📥 Ingesting Notes (Multi-File, .rtf, .txt, .md, .csv, .json)

Both Verb Lab and Vocabulary Studio feature dedicated note ingestion studios with full multi-file support:
* **Batch Multiple Files Drop & Select:** Drag and drop or browse multiple files simultaneously (e.g. `Week1_Verbs.rtf`, `Week2_Irregulars.md`, `Week3_Vocabulary.txt`).
* **Concurrent Asynchronous Decoding:** Reads, decodes, and parses all dropped files simultaneously with live status tracking and individual file pill badges.
* **Smart Deduplication & Source Tracking:** Merges identical entries across multiple files, preserving the richest gloss while tagging each word with its originating source file(s) in the interactive review table.
* **Rich Text (.rtf) Native Support:** Full built-in decoding of macOS TextEdit / Microsoft Word `.rtf` files, converting Unicode character codes (including Hindi Devanagari) to plain text automatically.
* **Smart Verb Parser:** Extracts verbs ending in *-ना*, isolates stems, and infers transitivity ('ने' rule).
* **Smart Vocabulary Parser:** Extracts nouns and adjectives, detects gender markers (`[m]`, `[f]`), infers gender from word endings (-ी &rarr; Feminine, -ा &rarr; Masculine), and prepares vocabulary cards.
* **Local Persistence & Backup:** Saves custom entries directly to browser `localStorage` and provides one-click **"Export Database (JSON)"** backup buttons for both decks.
